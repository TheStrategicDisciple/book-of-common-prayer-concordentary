*The Collect.*

**O** ALMIGHTY God, who alone canst order the unruly wills and affections of
sinful men; Grant unto thy people, that they may love the thing which
thou commandest, and desire that which thou dost promise; that so,
among the sundry and manifold changes of the world, our hearts may
surely there be fixed, where true joys are to be found; through Jesus
Christ our Lord. *Amen*.
