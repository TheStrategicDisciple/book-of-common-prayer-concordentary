*The Collect.*

**A**LMIGHTY God, who hast given thine only Son to be unto us both a
sacrifice for sin, and also an ensample of godly life; Give us grace
that we may always most thankfully receive that his inestimable benefit,
and also daily endeavour ourselves to follow the blessed steps of his
most holy life; through the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord. *Amen*.
