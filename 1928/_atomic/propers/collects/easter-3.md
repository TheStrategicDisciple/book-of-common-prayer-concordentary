*The Collect.*

**A**LMIGHTY God, who showest to them that are in error the light of thy
truth, to the intent that they may return into the way of righteousness;
Grant unto all those who are admitted into the fellowship of Christ's
Religion, that they may avoid those things that are contrary to their
profession, and follow all such things as are agreeable to the same;
through our Lord Jesus Christ. *Amen*.
